Privacy Policy

Learn how Lumina Dental Clinic collects, utilizes, safeguards, and protects your personal and healthcare information.

Home / Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Lumina Dental Clinic ("we", "our", or "the Clinic") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of confidentiality and security regarding our patients' personal and medical data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, and disclosing information collected via this website and our clinical reception.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you interact with our website, request appointments, or consult with our staff:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, email address, telephone numbers, and residential address.
  • Appointment Request Details: Desired appointment dates, preferred clinicians, and service interests.
  • Medical & Dental Health Intake: Relevant dental history, symptoms, allergies, and treatment preferences submitted with your consent.
  • Technical Browsing Data: IP address, device operating system, browser type, and interaction timestamps.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate healthcare and communication operations, including:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and coordinating clinical appointments.
  • Providing accurate, individualized clinical dental assessments.
  • Responding directly to inquiries submitted through our contact channels.
  • Improving our website performance and patient communication interfaces.

4. Information Sharing & Non-Sale Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize patient personal data under any circumstances. Information is only disclosed to authorized clinical team members, certified dental laboratories for prosthetic fabrication, or when required by healthcare statutory law.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses standard essential cookies to optimize navigation performance and remember session preferences. You can adjust your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies at any time without impacting core website accessibility.

6. Data Security & Electronic Storage

We implement robust technical, administrative, and physical safeguards, including SSL/TLS encryption for all web traffic and restricted-access electronic health management databases, to protect your sensitive data against unauthorized disclosure or loss.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain external links to social networks or partner professional bodies. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices or content of third-party domains.

8. Patient & User Rights

You maintain the right to:

  • Request access to the personal records we hold regarding your care.
  • Request rectification of incomplete or inaccurate personal information.
  • Request restriction or deletion of non-statutory records where legally permissible.

9. Children's Privacy

We provide pediatric dental services for children; however, online appointment bookings and personal data for minors must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect technological or regulatory advancements. The revision date at the top of this page indicates the most recent update.
