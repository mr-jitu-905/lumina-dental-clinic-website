Foundation of Health

Why Routine General Dentistry Is Essential

Regular dental examinations do more than clean your teeth; they allow for early interception of potential oral issues like cavities, gum inflammation, and enamel wear before they require extensive intervention.

At Lumina Dental Clinic, our general dental procedures use gentle ultrasonic instruments, high-magnification loupes, and biological materials that preserve as much of your natural tooth structure as possible.

Our Preventive Standards

  • Comprehensive 32-Point Oral Exam Full evaluation of gums, teeth, bite, jaw joints, and oral tissues.
  • Ultrasonic Gentle Scaling Painless removal of hardened tartar and bio-film without enamel damage.
  • Bio-Compatible Restorations BPA-free resin composite fillings color-matched to your natural teeth.
Core Treatments

General Dental Procedures

Our comprehensive range of restorative and preventive therapies designed to maintain your natural smile for a lifetime.

Check-ups & Cleanings

Regular preventive exams, digital low-dose X-rays, ultrasonic scaling, and air polishing to keep teeth and gums in prime health.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Seamless composite resin fillings that repair tooth decay, bond tightly with enamel, and blend invisibly with your natural tooth shade.

Root Canal Therapy

Gentle, pain-relieving endodontic treatments utilizing rotary instruments and apex locators to save badly infected or damaged teeth.

Crowns & Bridges

Custom monolithic zirconia and porcelain restorations designed to rebuild weakened teeth or bridge gaps caused by missing teeth.

Periodontal Gum Care

Deep scaling and root planing, antimicrobial irrigation, and supportive gum therapy to prevent and treat gingivitis and periodontitis.

Gentle Extractions

Atraumatic surgical and simple extractions for non-restorable teeth or impacted wisdom teeth with rapid recovery protocols.

Long-Term Benefits

Why Regular General Dentistry Matters

Consistent preventative visits do more than keep teeth clean. They safeguard systemic health, save you from costly emergency procedures, and preserve natural chewing efficiency.

Prevents Tooth Loss

Detecting periodontal disease and microscopic decay early stops tooth loss before irreversible damage occurs.

Protects Cardiovascular & General Health

Eliminating chronic gum inflammation lowers systemic bacteria linked to heart disease and diabetes.

Saves Time & Long-Term Expenses

Preventive maintenance is significantly more economical than complex oral surgery or dental bridges later.
