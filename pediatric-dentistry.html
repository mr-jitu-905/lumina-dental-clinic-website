Caring for Young Smiles

A Positive, Fear-Free Dental Foundation

We understand that early dental experiences shape how children perceive oral health for the rest of their lives. Our pediatric dental suite is designed to be joyful, friendly, and completely anxiety-free.

Led by Dr. Elena Rostova, our team uses child-friendly terminology, positive reinforcement, and gentle techniques to make dental examinations an adventure rather than a chore.

Services for Kids

Comprehensive Pediatric Treatments

Preventive therapies and gentle interventions tailored for primary and adolescent permanent teeth.

First Dental Check-Up

Gentle introductory examinations recommended by age one to track tooth eruption and guide parents on oral care.

Cavity Dental Sealants

Protective resin coatings placed on deep grooves of molars to physically seal out food and prevent 80% of cavities.

Fluoride Enamel Varnish

Quick, painless application of mineral-rich fluoride that strengthens developing enamel against sugar acids.

Early Orthodontic Guidance

Monitoring jaw development and spacing to identify bite misalignment or crossbites early and simplify future braces.

Habit Counseling

Supportive strategies to gently guide children away from thumb sucking, tongue thrusting, or extended bottle use.

Pediatric Emergency Care

Urgent care for dental bumps, chipped baby teeth, toothaches, or playground dental injuries with rapid comfort relief.

Parent Support

Tips for Parents

How you can help your child feel relaxed, confident, and prepared for dental visits.

1

Keep Language Positive

Avoid words like "hurt", "needle", or "shot". Use reassuring phrases like "checking your strong smile".

2

Schedule When Well-Rested

Morning appointments are best for younger children when they are energized and naturally cooperative.

3

Practice at Home

Count teeth together in the mirror so opening their mouth for the dentist feels natural and familiar.

4

Reward Bravery

Celebrate small milestones. Every child leaves Lumina with a bravery badge and a healthy smile kit.
