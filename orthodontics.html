Healthy Alignment

Straighter Teeth for Lifetime Oral Function

Proper tooth alignment is crucial for more than just aesthetics. Properly aligned teeth reduce excessive wear, alleviate jaw joint stress (TMJ), eliminate hard-to-clean food traps, and protect gum health long-term.

At Lumina Dental Clinic, our orthodontists utilize cutting-edge digital 3D scans to map out precise millimeter-level movements, providing you with predictable treatment timelines and comfortable appliance options.

Treatment Systems

Orthodontic Solutions for Every Lifestyle

Customized alignment therapies tailored for children, active teenagers, and working professionals.

Invisible Clear Aligners

Virtually imperceptible, removable polyurethane trays customized to shift teeth incrementally without metal brackets.

  • 100% Removable for meals
  • Smooth & comfortable fit
  • Fewer clinic visits required

Discreet Ceramic Braces

Tooth-colored ceramic brackets that blend naturally with your enamel for fixed orthodontic power with minimal visual impact.

  • Color-matched brackets
  • Handles complex corrections
  • Stain-resistant ceramic

Self-Ligating Braces

Low-friction specialized brackets that move teeth more quickly and comfortably with reduced tightening appointments.

  • Low friction biomechanics
  • Easier daily hygiene
  • Accelerated movement

Post-Treatment Retainers

Durable clear retainers and bonded lingual wires to lock your teeth into their optimal positions and preserve results permanently.

  • Clear Hawley & Essix types
  • Invisible fixed wires
  • Lifetime smile stability
Candidate Profiles

Who Can Benefit from Orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatment is highly effective at almost any stage of life.

Adult Professionals

Seeking discreet, aesthetic alignment without disrupting business meetings, social occasions, or personal lifestyle.

Teens & Adolescents

Correcting overcrowding, crossbites, and space gaps during crucial facial growth phases for lifelong stability.

Overbite & Underbite

Re-establishing healthy bite mechanics to relieve uneven enamel wear and tension in the jaw joints.

Relapsed Smiles

Quick re-alignment for individuals who previously had braces in the past but experienced minor shifting over time.

Treatment Roadmap

Your Orthodontic Journey Timeline

What to expect from your first 3D scan to your final retention retainer.

Stage 1

3D Digital Scan

Comprehensive intraoral optical scan and facial bite photography.

Stage 2

Virtual Plan

Review 3D simulation of planned tooth movements and milestones.

Stage 3

Placement

Fitting your first aligner trays or custom ceramic brackets.

Stage 4

Progress Check

Routine 6-8 week check-ins to monitor alignment progression.

Stage 5

Retention

Custom retainers to preserve your aligned smile permanently.
