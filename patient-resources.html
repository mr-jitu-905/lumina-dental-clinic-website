Daily Wellness

Helpful Tips for Everyday Oral Care

Simple everyday habits that protect your teeth between clinical visits.

Brush Twice for 2 Minutes

Use a soft-bristled brush with fluoride toothpaste at a 45-degree angle toward the gumline.

Floss Daily Before Bed

Cleaning between teeth removes trapped plaque that toothbrush bristles simply cannot reach.

Hydrate with Water

Drinking water stimulates saliva production, neutralizing acids and protecting enamel naturally.

Replace Your Toothbrush

Swap out your brush or electric head every 3 months or as soon as bristles begin to fray.
